## SEO Anti-Patterns (DO NOT USE)

- No "In today's rapidly evolving digital landscape..."
- No "underscores the importance of..."
- No "serves as a testament to..."
- No "Whether you're a beginner or expert..."
- No "Let's dive in!" or "Here's what you need to know"
- No emojis in headings or professional copy
- No generic positive conclusions
- No vague attributions ("Experts say...", "Industry reports...")
